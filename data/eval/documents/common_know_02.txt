Photosynthesis is the fundamental biological process through which autotrophic organisms, such as green plants, algae, and cyanobacteria, convert radiant solar energy into stable chemical energy. Operating inside cellular chloroplasts, light energy excites chlorophyll pigments to synthesize adenosine triphosphate and reduce nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate, driving the assimilation of carbon dioxide and water into organic carbohydrates while releasing molecular oxygen as a byproduct.
