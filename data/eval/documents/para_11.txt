Graph neural networks extend deep learning principles to non-grid relational structures where information is mapped as vertices and edges. The core computing paradigm relies on iterative message passing, in which every node synthesizes features from adjacent neighboring nodes over successive hidden layers. An updating step then integrates these aggregated inputs with the node's prior embedding. Permutation invariance ensures that graph isomorphisms yield identical outputs. Nonetheless, deeper graph architectures often suffer from over-smoothing, wherein embeddings of disparate nodes blend into uniform vectors, restricting useful model depth in molecular analysis and social networks.
