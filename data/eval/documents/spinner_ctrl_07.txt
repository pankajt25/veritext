Autonomous robotic navigation across unstructured agricultural terrain requires real-time sensor fusion and high-speed motor control algorithms. Autonomous tractors utilize computer vision perception stacks to distinguish harvestable crops from invasive weed vegetation reliably. Deep learning convolutional backbones process high-resolution multispectral imagery, feeding obstacle coordinates into trajectory planning modules. Simultaneously, location telemetry from localized wheel encoders and satellite receivers updates terrain elevation maps on edge processors. Deploying random forest decision models ensures that the vehicle can respond safely to unexpected physical hazards without human intervention.
