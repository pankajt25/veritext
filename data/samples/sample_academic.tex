\section{Formal Formulation}
Let $S = \{s_1, s_2, \dots, s_n\}$ represent an ordered sequence of sentences. The cosine similarity between two sentence vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ is given by:

\begin{equation}
\text{Sim}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|}
\end{equation}

For optimization, we apply thresholding where $T \in [0.75, 0.95]$.
