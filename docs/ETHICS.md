# Ethics, Responsible Use & Academic Integrity Policy

## 1. Core Mission & Philosophy
Veritext is designed to support educators, researchers, and students by providing transparent, inspectable, and open-source tools for textual analysis. Our mission is to encourage genuine learning, academic growth, and fair inquiry—**not to automate disciplinary action or punitive surveillance**.

Text analysis tools are diagnostic instruments that highlight patterns requiring human attention. They do not possess moral judgment, intent detection, or legal authority.

---

## 2. Fundamental Limitations of AI-Written Text Detection

### 2.1 Probabilistic Nature
No automated AI detector can achieve 100% accuracy. Large Language Models (LLMs) and human writers draw from overlapping linguistic distributions. Predictions generated by stylometric analysis, language model perplexity, or neural classifiers represent **statistical likelihoods**, not incontrovertible proof.

### 2.2 Disproportionate False-Positive Impact on Non-Native (ESL) Writers
Extensive research and empirical evaluations demonstrate that non-native English speakers consistently face elevated false-positive rates when evaluated by AI detectors:
- ESL writers often employ simpler, more uniform sentence structures, lower syntactic variance, and formulaic transition phrases (e.g., "Furthermore", "In conclusion", "Moreover").
- Statistical detectors trained to flag low burstiness and regular sentence length may misclassify genuine, human-written ESL compositions as machine-generated.

> [!CAUTION] Mandatory Protection for ESL Students
> In Veritext, conservative classification thresholds and confidence intervals are explicitly tuned to minimize false accusations against non-native writers. **Under no circumstances should an AI detection probability alone be used to accuse a student of academic dishonesty.**

---

## 3. Policy Recommendations for Educators & Institutions

1. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Review**: Every flag raised by Veritext (whether for verbatim plagiarism, text-spinning, or AI text) must be reviewed by an instructor with contextual domain expertise.
2. **Conversational Inquiries Over Punitive Measures**: If an essay raises integrity concerns, instructors should initiate an open, non-accusatory discussion with the student regarding their writing process, drafts, citations, and conceptual reasoning.
3. **Transparent Criteria**: Students should be informed in course syllabi if textual integrity tools are utilized, and instructors should openly explain what the tools measure and their technical limitations.
4. **Draft and Version History Verification**: Genuine student work typically leaves version artifacts (e.g., commit logs, draft revisions, handwritten outlines). Instructors should consider developmental evidence before reaching conclusions.

---

## 4. Data Privacy, Confidentiality & Student Rights

- **Zero Commercial Harvesting**: Veritext never uploads, transmits, or sells student documents to third-party model developers, commercial brokers, or search engines.
- **Local-First Processing**: Ingested files, class repositories, review notes, and cryptographic signing keys reside exclusively on the user's local disk or private institutional server.
- **Compliance with Educational Privacy Standards**: By operating offline and keeping document processing local, Veritext adheres to the strictest privacy principles under FERPA, GDPR, and institutional data retention regulations.
